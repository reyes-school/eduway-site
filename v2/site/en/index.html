    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    What is EduWay?

    EduWay is a school and kindergarten management platform. It brings attendance, grades, homework, timetables, payments, salaries, reports and parent communication into one system. It works as a website and as Android and iOS apps.

    What does “under the school's brand” mean?

    EduWay builds a dedicated version for each school: the school's logo, colors and name, its own domain, and an app published in the App Store and Google Play under the school's name. Parents and teachers see your school's brand, not EduWay.

    Which roles does the platform have?

    Six roles: head admin (principal), manager, accountant, teacher, preschool teacher and parent. Each role has its own dashboard and permissions — for example, a parent sees only their own child and an accountant sees only their branch's finances.

    What do parents see in the app?

    Their child's attendance, grades, class ranking, today's lessons and topics, homework, menu, payments and school news. Parents can send a “My child can't come” notice and rate teachers.

    Is there a mobile app?

    Yes. The platform works on iPhone (iOS), Android and desktop browsers, with the same data on every device. The app is published in the App Store and Google Play with your school's name and logo.

    How long does launch take?

    Usually 10 business days: discovery (days 1–2), brand and setup (days 3–5), data import (days 6–8), staff training and go-live (days 9–10).

    Do we have to enter student lists ourselves?

    No. During launch we import student, teacher and parent data for you.

    Is the data secure?

    Yes. Documents and personal data are encrypted with AES-256, passwords are stored as hashes, login is locked after repeated failed attempts, important actions are written to an audit log and backups run daily. Data can also be hosted on servers in Uzbekistan.

    What if we have several branches and a kindergarten?

    EduWay supports several branches in one organisation, and each branch can have a school and a kindergarten section. The principal sees the overall report; staff see only their own branch.

    Which languages are supported?

    The interface and PDF reports are available in Uzbek (Latin) and Russian. Each user picks their language.

    How much does it cost?

    Pricing is calculated for each school individually. Fill in the form on the site (your name, phone, school name and region) and our specialist will contact you with an exact offer. Every plan includes all modules.

    Is there a free demo?

    Yes. We'll show you the platform live using your own school as the example. Leave a request using the form below.

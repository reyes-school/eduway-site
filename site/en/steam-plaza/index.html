Steam Plaza: a school platform under its own brand

Steam Plaza is the first school built on EduWay. The platform runs with the Steam Plaza logo and colors as a website and Android and iOS apps.

About the school

Steam Plaza is an education organisation with two branches, each with a school and a kindergarten. The platform therefore had to work per branch and per section: an accountant sees only their branch, while the principal sees the whole organisation.

The challenge

  • Move attendance, grades, homework, payments and parent communication from paper, Excel and chats into one system.
  • Give parents everything about their child in a single app.
  • Show leadership the real state of the school — lessons, finance, debtors — in time.
  • Ship it all under the school's own brand.

The solution

  • EduWay was prepared for Steam Plaza as a dedicated version: the Steam Plaza name, logo and green brand color. The app identifier and store listing belong to the school too.
  • Separate dashboards went live for five roles: principal, manager, accountant, teacher and parent.

Modules launched

  • Timetable, lessons, attendance, grades and homework
  • Payments, salaries, expenses, debtors, monthly and annual reports
  • AI reading of bank payment orders
  • Student and teacher PDF profiles (with documents inside)
  • News, open lessons and menu
  • “My child can't come” notices and push notifications
  • Student and teacher rankings
  • Audit log
